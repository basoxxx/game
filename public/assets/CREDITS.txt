Territorio (src/territorio): no external assets. Board, vehicles, obstacles and effects are procedural
(three.js primitives and shaders); the cover public/covers/territorio.webp is rendered from the game.
Serpentone: no third-party assets. Arenas, snakes, hats, food and the cover (covers/serpentone.webp) are procedural, rendered by the game itself.
Minigolf Baso (src/minigolf/): all course geometry, props, textures (canvas) and sounds are procedural - no external assets.
Bastione: no external assets. Models, maps, icons, effects and sounds are procedural (three.js primitives,
canvas, inline SVG, Web Audio); the bean figures reuse Orda's procedural geometry.
Kart Baso (src/kart): no third-party assets. Karts, drivers, items, tracks, props and textures are built
procedurally in code (three.js primitives and canvas textures); sounds come from the hub's procedural
audio engine (src/ui/audio.js). Characters, tracks and items are original creations.
Ingorgo (src/ingorgo): no external assets. Vehicles, city, textures (windows, water, chequers) and
all sounds (horns, engines) are generated in code; levels come from the seeded generator + solver.

Officina: no external assets. Garage, track, parts, crates and the kart are procedural (three.js); the race
cars are Apex Dynasty's own procedural models (src/ui/cars). Cover rendered from the game (tools/officina-cover.mjs).

Festa Baso (src/festa): no external assets. The board, minigame arenas, effects and the cover
(covers/festa.webp, rendered by tools/festa-cover.mjs) are procedural; it reuses the Baso Hub's own procedural
art (Orda figures, Serpentone hats and heads, Kart Baso karts, Scontro Totale bangers, Bastione buildings).

Rompicapo (src/rompicapo): no external assets. Boards, tiles (original Mahjong faces: suns, drops, leaves,
winds, gems, flowers, seasons), bricks, effects and sounds are drawn and synthesised in code; the cover
(covers/rompicapo.webp) is rendered from the games by tools/rompicapo-cover.mjs.
